## Decisions

- **DMF topic:** Letter U (day 21), topic "Under" — avalanche buries; survivor is under the weight
- **CNN headline:** Deadly California avalanche kills at least 8 backcountry skiers; 6 were close friends (February 21, 2026)
- **Protagonist:** Delphine Osei-Bonsu, 31, Ghanaian-American PE teacher — differs from recent protagonists in age bracket, profession, cultural background, and story shape (grief without a villain, no institutional backdrop)
- **Tense/POV:** Third person past tense, omniscient — most common recent tense is present (3 of last 5); most common POV is first and third tied; omniscient has never been used in the project, making it the most distinct choice
- **Title:** "Names" — plural noun, no article, resonant double meaning
- **Story shape:** Grief without a villain, natural disaster, domestic/relational — varies from the recent streak of professional/institutional dilemmas
- **Protocol change:** Added one sentence to Notes after the metaphor annotation rule: "When a climactic action derives its meaning from a specific detail — the exact word, the particular order, the chosen object — cut any following clause that explains what that detail means; the specificity is the argument." Motivated by the draft including an explanatory clause after Delphine writes six names in meeting-order; the clause was cut, the rule added.

## Learnings

- Third person omniscient (never used before) worked well for this story's structure: Alcazar's interiority gives the reader both sides of the threshold scene without requiring a POV rupture
- "All of them?" as the climactic dialogue beat demonstrates that the question-as-confirmation structure (character already knows, asks to be told) can carry enormous weight with minimal words
- The Marcus characterization in two clauses ("who had hated skiing the first three years...and that was fine, that was Marcus") showed that dead characters can be individuated powerfully in interior monologue — they don't need to appear on the page to be present
- Ended the story with three people in the frame (Delphine, young ranger, Alcazar) to avoid "protagonist alone in final paragraph" watchlist item while keeping the weight of Alcazar's witness
