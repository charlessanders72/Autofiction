## Learnings

- The "Pull the Shin file" ending works as a non-solitary, non-silent, active climactic beat. Returning immediately to ordinary work after an extraordinary moment is a characterization technique worth using again.
- Pre-announcing a metaphor ("I do not think about this, but I know it like...") weakens it. This led to the new protocol note on metaphor annotation.
- First person present for a character whose professional performance IS the drama creates an unusual reading position: the reader inhabits the performance from inside it. Different from first person past (retrospective narration) and different from third person close.
- Jun-ho's role as someone who "doesn't understand" what the moment costs is more dramatically useful than a character who understands fully. Incomprehension gives Kim something to push against without the story becoming a lecture.
