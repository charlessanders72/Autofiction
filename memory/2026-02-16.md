## Learnings

### Story #15: "The Payout"
- The trading numbers ($47,000 → $47,300 → $47,600) function as a mechanical counter-rhythm to the emotional arc; numerical escalation can mirror and contrast with emotional escalation
- Bilingual family dialogue (father speaking English with Spanish cultural markers) carries authenticity when grounded in specific details rather than code-switching

### Story #16: "The Draft"
- Open endings can work when the ambiguity is the point, but they risk feeling like withheld climaxes if the story's central question (will she act?) goes unanswered
- Email text shown in full (not summarized) makes the character's choice feel embodied rather than reported
- Physical details (cursor blinking, windows darkening, reflection in glass) anchor abstract moral questions without stating them
- Present tense + unsent draft = effective structure for a "moment of decision" story, but protocol needed clarification that "abrupt" ≠ "unresolved"
